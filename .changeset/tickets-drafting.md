**Create ticket…** can now draft with Critiqs AI and from the team's own Linear templates. **Draft with Critiqs AI**, with the model's cost beside it, writes the title and text from the thread, and the chosen template, for the person to edit; it follows the project's AI usage rule and counts credits like search and mockups, and when AI is off, out of credits, rate limited, or the provider fails, the draft is filled without AI and says why. A project's owners turn on **Use the team's ticket templates** in Tags and AI's new **Tickets** tab (off by default) and choose which of their Linear teams' templates to offer; the dialog's **Template** menu then fills the draft from one, read from Linear each time, and the issue is created from it so Linear applies its other defaults. Up to five Linear labels whose names match the thread's tags, never two of one single-select label group, are suggested as removable chips and added to the issue. Critiqs AI never reads the page's address, view state, or the thread's link; Critiqs AI writes plain text by section, which Critiqs arranges under the template's headings, each once and in the template's order, with every Markdown character escaped, so nothing it writes renders as a link, address, image, HTML, or code; Critiqs adds the thread's link itself. A ticket is created with the person's text exactly as they left it, with a closing fence added only when it ends inside a code fence, so the capture and attribution after it render. When another team's draft can't load, Create waits and offers **Try again**. The Convex backend gains `tickets:options`, `tickets:templateSettings`, `tickets:saveTemplateSettings`, `tickets:trackerTemplates`, and `ai:draftTicket`; `tickets:draft` takes an optional `destinationId` and `templateId`, and `tickets:create` an optional `templateId` and `labelIds`. These appear only where `/critiqs/config` reports `tickets.drafting`; older backends keep today's dialog.
