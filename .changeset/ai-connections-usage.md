On a deployment with plans, **Your AI** shows the Critiqs AI credits you've used against your own plan when Critiqs AI is chosen, such as "Today: 12 of 500 credits on your Team plan.", or says when your plan doesn't include personal Critiqs AI. A project's plan usage now lists its AI as **Critiqs AI credits** instead of "AI requests". Backends gain `aiSettings:myUsage` (Convex) and `personalAiUsage` (self-hosted server); a backend without the call shows no usage.
