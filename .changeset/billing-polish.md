Organization and billing fixes. Inviting someone says "Invite sent" only once the mail service accepted the email; where no email went out, as on a deployment without email, it says they join by signing in with that address, and where the email is still to be sent, as on Convex, it just says they're invited (`inviteMember` answers carry `email`: `sent`, `not-sent`, or `pending`; absent from older backends). On the Organization page, each action shows its own error beside what it acted on, including a refused withdrawal beside its invite, and an action that finishes after you moved to another page or organization, or closed the dialog, neither reports nor moves you; the Plan row opens Plan and billing where the deployment takes payment, or links to Upgrade where it names an upgrade page (organizations report `upgradeUrl`). Where the deployment takes payment, Upgrade appears only once the organization it opens is known to have a higher plan that Change plan offers, so never on Pro or while a Pro subscription is billed. Plan and billing says when each removed storage add-on ends, by its own period, and, where that end lowers it, the image storage left then under the plan in effect, saying no amount while the order of the ends and a plan change isn't known (billing status reports `endingStorageAddOns`). A refused Critiqs AI request says whether no credits are left or not enough are left for that request. `setOverageCap` uses the Organization page's admin check and wording, and still refuses everyone who isn't an admin with `forbidden`.
