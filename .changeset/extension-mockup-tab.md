The Critiqs browser extension offers Mockup mode: its side panel opens Mockup mode in a tab of its own beside the page, which freezes the page and runs the Mockup panel there. The panel gains an internal `mockups` option for this (Mockup mode opened elsewhere, or a window for Mockup mode alone, where holding O to compare isn't offered). Nothing changes for host apps.
