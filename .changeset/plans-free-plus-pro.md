Critiqs Cloud's plans are now Free, Plus, and Pro. Each organization gets a monthly allowance of Critiqs AI credits (`aiCredits`), shared by its projects and by its members' own Critiqs AI. Plus and Pro can use extra credits past the allowance, up to a cap the organization's admins set through the new `setOverageCap` call (reported as the `overageCap` capability), or put back to the plan's default with `"default"`; a change of plan puts it back too. Pro organizations can add image storage in 100 GB steps. When an organization's included credits run out, and when none are left, its admins get one email each month and plan. Usage reports gain `overage` on limits that allow extra and an `unlimited` list for what the plan doesn't limit; the setup panel and Your AI show both. Components ask for the credits row as `aiCredits` with the new `aiCredits` argument; older components get it as `aiRequests`, as before. Organizations on the former Team plan get Plus. Projects, images, and Critiqs AI credits now count per organization. Self-hosted SQLite databases migrate to schema 10 on start. On Convex, `organizations:backfill` now ends by recounting each organization's images from its projects (`organizations:recountStorage`, safe to run again).
