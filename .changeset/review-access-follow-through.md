Access to a project limited to its organization is now checked again where work that started earlier would hand data on: emails being prepared or retried aren't sent, and their captures aren't read, once the recipient can no longer review the project; search, Critiqs AI in mockups, and connection tests check right before each request to the model, retries included, so access that ends while credits are held sends nothing; a connection test or model list, saved or draft, by someone who stopped managing the project records and returns nothing; an upload under way is not saved; and a Convex screenshot or page copy whose access ends while it downloads is not returned. `/critiqs/health` describes such a project only to its members, so `critiqs doctor` says it can't check it instead of failing. Only a mockup's creator starts its thread. Deleting a mockup now follows the thread rule: its creator can't delete it while it has a thread someone else started. Linking another project's thread to a mockup is refused the same way whether it exists, was deleted, or is limited to its organization. Threads report `canDelete`, and the thread window offers **Delete thread** only to their author or the project's owners, closing only once the deletion succeeds. On Convex, screenshots (without R2) and page copies upload through the deployment's own routes, reserved for the uploader, project, and purpose, and can be attached only there, once, within an hour; unattached files are deleted after that. A person has at most 20 unfinished uploads, and a project 200 MB. Released components keep uploading page copies, through a one-use grant in the upload address; a file uploaded just before the update is uploaded again automatically, or, in released components, when the reviewer tries again.
