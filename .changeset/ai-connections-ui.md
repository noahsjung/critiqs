Each person can now connect their own AI from **Your AI…** in the avatar menu: Critiqs AI on their own plan, or their own key, tested before saving and removable at any time. In Tags and AI, a project's owner can choose who pays for AI (**Project pays**, **Each person pays**, or **Project pays, personal allowed**, the default), pick the project's Critiqs AI model with its credits, and limit which models the project's AI allows. When a mockup request or a search needs a person's own AI first, it offers **Set up your AI**. The project's connection choices now read **Critiqs AI** and **Your own key**. Your AI and the new choices appear only on backends that report `personalAi`; older backends keep today's settings.
