Escape no longer changes the mode. It closes what is innermost (a menu or dialog, a new comment, a replacement being chosen, an open thread) and then the side panel; C, V and M change modes. In Mockup mode, Escape puts down a chosen element and then closes the panel, which reopens on the same thread, and the panel's close button closes it instead of leaving Mockup mode. Hints say "V to leave".
