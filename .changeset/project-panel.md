The sidebar now follows the mode. In Browse mode it shows the project, titled with its name: open and unread threads with **Show comments**, who has access and who commented recently, your email notifications, plan usage for the owner, and the project ID with **Project setup**. In Comment mode it shows the threads under the title "Comments". Clicking a tag opens the threads in Comment mode. The toolbar's sidebar button is named after the panel it opens, and the Mockup button and shortcut are labeled "Mockup mode", like the other modes.
