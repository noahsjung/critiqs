Critiqs's panel can run beside a review link's frame: `mountPanel` takes a `review` option for the page around it, which owns Browse and Comment, signs a reviewer in when they post their first comment (keeping what they picked and wrote), and opens a thread at the frame width it was left at. In that mode the panel always applies the untrusted message limits, maps every address the frame reports to the prototype's own and refuses others, and sends threads to the frame only as aliases with a generic name. `Session` gains `acceptPanelSignIn` for tokens from Critiqs's own panel sign-in. Installed Critiqs and the extension's panel behave as before. A project's owners also reach its review links from the panel's project setup (**Manage review links**), when the deployment serves review links and reports its website. On a narrow screen the review page shows the panel as a sheet over the frame's lower part (the `review` option's `panel`), opened from the page's bar and closed with its own button. In Comment mode on a touch screen (no hover, a coarse pointer), the picking hint names a tap instead of keys.
