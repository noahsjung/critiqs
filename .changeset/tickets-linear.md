Reviewers can now turn a thread into a Linear issue, or link it to one, from the thread window's **⋯** menu: **Create ticket…** fills the title and text from the thread for editing, remembers the team per person and project, uploads the capture into the issue, and adds "Created from Critiqs" unless turned off; **Link ticket…** searches the person's Linear by key or words and can add a comment with the thread's summary. Both use the person's own account from Your trackers and open it first when none is connected. Linked tickets show with their key and status under the thread window's header (opening the ticket, with **Unlink** for the person who linked it or a project owner), on the thread's row in the comments list, on its pin, and on comments added to a mockup. A thread links to at most 20 tickets, and a ticket being created or linked holds its place before anything is written to Linear; one Critiqs couldn't confirm stops counting after 10 minutes and can still be finished, and a thread holds at most 40. Each Create dialog's tries are one submission (`submissionId`, a UUID v4): while one attempt is creating the issue, another is told to try again in a moment, and a retry, even after a lost answer, finds or returns the issue rather than making a second; only the person who created a ticket recovers it, with their own Linear account, even when the issue was archived since. A ticket the person started on a thread that didn't finish is finished by their next **Create ticket…** there, which says so; the draft names it (`resumeSubmissionId`), and a new submission is refused until it is finished. Every call to Linear uses the token of the Linear account its request checked, so connecting another account meanwhile stops it. When Linear doesn't add the link back to the thread, the notice and the ticket's chip offer **Add link back** to its creator (`canAddLinkBack`). Linking comments at most once. The ticket menu items appear only where `/critiqs/config` reports `tickets.threadTickets`, and linked tickets in thread answers carry their tracker ID (`externalId`). Thread answers now say whether the person may unlink each ticket (`canUnlink`). The Convex backend gains `tickets:destinations`, `tickets:search`, `tickets:draft`, `tickets:create`, `tickets:link`, `tickets:addLinkBack`, and `tickets:unlink`; backends without tickets, including the self-hosted SQLite server, show none of this.
