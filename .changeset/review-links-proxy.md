The page layer's protocol can now bound what it takes from a page layer in a frame Critiqs doesn't trust, such as a review link's prototype, so one forged message can't exhaust the panel's tab: entries, keys, occurrences across a whole state, and the address's scheme (http or https only) are all bounded. Installed Critiqs keeps taking what its host app reports, as before. Host apps don't need to do anything. Inside a review link's frame, Critiqs installed by the prototype itself now stays out quietly, since the review page runs Critiqs around the frame; installed Critiqs anywhere else is unchanged.
