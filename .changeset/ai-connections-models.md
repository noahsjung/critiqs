With your own key, in Your AI or a project's AI settings, **Load models** now lists the models the key can use, to search and tick the ones on offer and choose the **Default model**; a model's name can still be typed. **OpenRouter** joins the providers, at an address Critiqs sets. In Mockup mode, a **Model** menu beside Send chooses which of your AI's models answers when it offers several, remembered for each project. These appear only on backends that report `modelLists`; older backends keep today's typed model and no model menu.
