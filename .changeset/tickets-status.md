Linked Linear tickets now keep their status current, and projects can resolve threads when their tickets are done. On Convex, the operator's Linear app can send issue events to `POST /trackers/linear/webhook`: set `CRITIQS_LINEAR_WEBHOOK_SECRET` to the webhook's signing secret, and each delivery (256 KB at most, or it gets 413) is used only when its `Linear-Signature`, checked over the delivery's exact bytes, and its timestamp (within a minute) check out; later deliveries of older changes, and replays, change nothing, and a deleted issue stays Deleted. A workspace's deliveries may change linked tickets 300 times a minute; past that the route answers 429 and changes nothing. Opening a thread also checks tickets whose status is more than 5 minutes old (`ticketStatus:checkStatus`, at most once every 5 minutes per ticket), with the Linear account of the person who linked each one and never anyone else's; when that person has disconnected or can no longer see the ticket, the chip keeps the last status, marked "last known" (`statusStale` on linked tickets) from the moment Critiqs can't check it. Project owners turn on **Resolve threads when their tickets are done** in a new Tickets tab of Tags and AI (`ticketStatus:settings`, `ticketStatus:setResolveWhenDone`; off by default): a thread resolves when every linked ticket is done or canceled, says it was resolved when its tickets were done (`resolvedByTickets`), and isn't reopened when a ticket is; turning it on catches up the open threads whose tickets are all already done. The component uses all of this only where `/critiqs/config` reports `tickets.ticketStatus`; older components ignore the new fields. A check follows an issue moved to another team: the link takes its new key and page. Every background use of a person's stored token checks, where the token is read, that they may still review the project. The self-hosted SQLite server is unchanged.
