AI setup is simpler. Everyone has Critiqs AI with nothing to set up, on their plan's credits: the built-in Free plan now includes 50 personal credits a day, and under "Each person pays" someone who saved nothing uses Critiqs AI on their own plan instead of being asked to set up Your AI. Your AI and the project's AI settings are now short lists of settings that save at once: the model chosen by name (its cost shows in the menu), the plan's credits with Upgrade where the deployment sets `CRITIQS_UPGRADE_URL`, and Use your own API key, which sets up a key in three steps. Mockup mode's model menu shows models by name. Your AI has no Off choice any more: Use Critiqs AI instead removes a saved key.
