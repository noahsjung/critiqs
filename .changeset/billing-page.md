Critiqs Cloud customers see their organization's plan and pay for it in **Plan and billing**, in the avatar menu, on deployments that report `billing`. Members see the plan and its price, when it renews or ends, a pending cancel or change, a failed payment, this month's Critiqs AI credits with extra credits and the cap, projects, and images stored. Admins buy a plan with nothing billed (from Free, or a plan Critiqs set, which the page says) on Stripe Checkout, even with a card on file, which returns to the page and waits for the payment, allowing no other change meanwhile; move between Plus and Pro or back to Free after a page that names the organization and says what it charges and when; keep a plan they cancelled; set the cap on extra credits (a number, none, or the plan's, with its most in dollars); add or remove Pro's 100 GB storage; and open Stripe's portal. Every **Upgrade** button opens this page there, instead of `CRITIQS_UPGRADE_URL`, on the person's home organization from **Your AI** and the project's elsewhere. Prices come from Autumn's catalog (`plans.list`), which the backend keeps and reads again each day; until it has read them, the page shows the plans and what they include with no prices, and pauses changes that would charge the card on file (between Plus and Pro, adding storage), which `billing:changePlan` and `billing:setStorageAddOn` refuse as well, while Checkout, going back to Free, and removing storage still work. What each plan includes (Critiqs AI credits, projects, image storage, reviewers, comments, mention emails, or unlimited) comes from the deployment's plan configuration, which sets the limits. The Convex backend adds `organizations:usage` and `billing:loadPrices`; `billing:status` adds the billed plan as `subscription.plan`, the prices as `prices`, and what each plan includes as `plans`; `billing:changePlan` takes `from`, the plan the page showed as billed, and refuses when Autumn bills another; `aiSettings:myUsage` adds `organizationId`. The transport's optional `billing` gains `useUsage` and `loadPrices`, and its `changePlan` takes `from`. Deployments without `billing` are unchanged.
