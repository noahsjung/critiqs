Solid and SolidStart apps (Solid 1.9 or 2) can add Critiqs with `<Critiqs />` from `critiqs-solid`, or from `critiqs-solid/router` in apps with Solid Router so threads name routes such as `users/:id` and opening one navigates there, and Critiqs's setup adds it to `src/App.tsx` or SolidStart's `src/app.tsx` with the project ID read from `VITE_CRITIQS_PROJECT_ID`.
