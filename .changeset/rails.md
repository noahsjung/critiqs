Add Critiqs to Ruby on Rails apps with the new critiqs-rails gem: `<%= critiqs_tag %>` in the layout shows Critiqs wherever CRITIQS_PROJECT_ID is set and reports each page's route, and `bin/rails generate critiqs:install` or `critiqs init` adds it for you.
